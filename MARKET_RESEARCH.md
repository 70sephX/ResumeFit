# ResumeFit 2.0 — Market research / Product inspiration

Research checked October 2026. The observations below are high-level product insights. ResumeFit does not use source code, assets, brand identity or interface designs from these competitors.

### Teal (tealhq.com)
- Useful: Job Matcher connects a saved resume to a saved or pasted job listing, highlights relevant keywords and supports iterative career workflows.
- What ResumeFit borrows conceptually: rapid job-specific tailoring and reviewing matched requirements.
- Source: https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/12060992-using-the-job-matcher

### Jobscan (jobscan.co)
- Useful: emphasizes structured, ATS-related feedback and job-specific fit diagnostics.
- Difference: ResumeFit avoids unsupported ATS approval percentages and instead shows exact source evidence.
- Source: https://www.jobscan.co/blog/best-ai-resume-builders/

### Rezi (rezi.ai)
- Useful: job-description keyword targeting, missing-skill identification and focused bullet suggestions.
- Difference: ResumeFit prioritizes factual grounding and candidate-controlled edits before exporting.
- Source: https://www.rezi.ai/rezi-docs/ai-keyword-targeting-explained

### Huntr (huntr.co)
- Useful: a base resume, targeted versions by job, editable AI suggestions and an application pipeline.
- Difference: ResumeFit focuses only on truthful resume tailoring rather than copying a full job-tracking system.
- Source: https://help.huntr.co/en/articles/13005373-building-your-tailored-resume
- Source: https://help.huntr.co/en/articles/11535512-ai-review-walkthrough

## Product positioning

**Brand:** ResumeFit — *Tailor your resume, not your facts.*

**Personas:** graduating college students, new graduates, international students, early-career analysts/software candidates, applicants transitioning between adjacent roles.

**Promise:** “The strongest version of your true experience for the job you want.”

**Core differentiation:** evidence-first rewriting. Every proposed change is tied to the source resume, with an explicit distinction between requirements that are supported, partially supported or not evidenced. User accepts/edits before generating a job-specific Word file.

**MVP boundaries:** Upload and text parsing, JD requirement matching, before/after bullets, evidence and explanation, missing qualifications, saved runs, per-credit pricing. No full-design recreation of uploaded resume; no arbitrary ATS pass-rate or guaranteed interviews; no LinkedIn sync; no automatic job applications.

**Future opportunities:** managed accounts/storage, optional tracked resume versions per employer, feedback on weak bullet phrasing, guided quantified-impact questions, provider-side structured outputs / evaluations, PDF export, cover letters as a separately opted-in product, onboarding/privacy and formal billing.

**Ethics:** Do not overclaim employment outcomes. Present demo mode honestly. Respect users’ personal resume data and comply with relevant platform/consumer privacy regulations prior to commercial rollout.
